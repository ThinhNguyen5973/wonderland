HOW TO PLAY — you are the gardener. Hold the left mouse to pour water; water brings dead soil to life. Plant seeds ([2]) on living soil — the garden decides what sprouts. Feed the creatures: mushrooms cause molts, eat-me cakes grow them, drink-me bottles shrink them. Kneel (right-click) to inspect the ground. Carry small things ([4]). SIGN ([3]) the creature you want to keep — a signed death becomes a page in the Book ([B]). When the season is done, press the paper ([P]) to fix it forever. you cannot save them. you can only sign them.

A WAY TO PLAY
1. Pour water on dead ground until it lives. 2. Plant seeds on what lives. 3. Feed the creatures — mushrooms cause molts, cakes grow, bottles shrink, tea restores. 4. Sign the one you want to keep. 5. Carry the small to food, to places, to each other. 6. Press the paper when the season is done.
